Common questions

How do I play and earn memories?

Choose the box, yarn, or pillow and tap Play here. Interact with Nori, then finish the game. The Home screen shows the condition for earning progress. New moments appear in Memories; earned accessories can be chosen in Appearance.

Why did the system card not appear?

The in-app game works independently of Live Activities. Check the Live Activities permission for NORI in iOS Settings if that switch is available. iOS controls how the card appears and refreshes; permanent presence and continuous animation are not promised.

Where are my cat and progress saved?

Game information is saved locally on your device. The app and its extension share a local data group. NORI has no cloud account or developer-operated recovery service for deleted saves.

Do not delete the app expecting support to restore your save. Apple device backups and restoration depend on your own system settings.

What should I include in a support email?

Your NORI version, device model, iOS version, and a description of what happened. You may attach a screenshot after hiding personal information. Do not send passwords, verification codes, your Apple ID, or your entire game database.

Your information

The free version of NORI has no advertising, analytics, accounts, or purchases. Your cat's information is not sent to a developer server. If you contact support, the developer receives only the information you choose to send.

When you visit this site, your browser connects to its hosting provider, which processes technical requests. This page has no analytics scripts, forms, or advertising integrations. This support information is not a substitute for the app's published privacy policy, which will be linked separately.
