Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy covers NORI: Virtual Pet Cat (com.casdreamesios.noviapp) and its public support pages. The developer responsible for this policy is Maksim Sokolov, an individual in the Russian Federation. Contact: msokolov2589@gmail.com.

Information kept on your device

Your pet, optional nickname, appearance, play sessions, progress, memories, and settings are stored locally on your device. You do not need an account. The app and its extension share a local Apple App Group container to keep their information consistent. The app does not upload this game information to a developer server.

No advertising or tracking

The current free version has no advertisements, tracking, analytics SDKs, third-party crash-reporting SDKs, purchases, or subscriptions. The app does not automatically transmit device identifiers or gameplay diagnostics to the developer. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health information.

Live Activities and Apple services

Information displayed in a Live Activity, widget, or Lock Screen can be seen by anyone who can see that screen. Your pet's nickname is hidden on system presentations by default. NORI has no developer-operated cloud account or synchronization service. Apple may handle device backup, restoration, operating-system diagnostics, and App Store services under your settings and Apple's own policies.

Voluntary support requests

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any details or attachments you decide to send. Contacting support is optional and is not required to play. NORI does not send email automatically or attach your save. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, Apple ID credentials, or an entire game database. A parent or guardian should handle support requests for a child.

How support information is used and shared

Information you send is used to answer your request, investigate the reported problem, and maintain necessary correspondence. It is not used for advertising, marketing messages, or sale. Support email is processed through Gmail/Google; that provider processes messages and connection information under its own terms and privacy policy. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security. Where applicable, processing is based on responding to your request and legitimate interests in providing support, or your consent where required by law.

External websites and hosting

Opening a support or privacy link connects your browser to its hosting provider. NORI does not add your pet name, save, or play history to the link. These public pages are hosted on GitHub/GitHub Pages, whose provider may process IP addresses and other technical request information under its own privacy policy. The NORI pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising integrations, or contact forms. Any cookies or features of the surrounding GitHub website are controlled by GitHub. Following an external link is subject to that destination’s practices. Email and hosting providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Retention and deletion

Local game information is kept so your pet and progress remain available when you return. The current app has no reset, export, or account-deletion screen. Deleting the app rather than offloading it normally removes its local data; a shared container can remain while another installed member of the same App Group still uses it. Device backups may retain separate copies under your Apple settings. The developer cannot access or erase your local save or Apple-controlled backups, and cannot recover a deleted save from a developer server. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle the request, keep necessary records, or meet legal obligations.

Your privacy requests

You can contact msokolov2589@gmail.com to ask about information you sent to support or to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable law and any necessary retention. Please identify the correspondence without sending unnecessary identity documents. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. These requests do not give the developer access to your on-device game data.

Children’s information

The app does not ask for an age, require personal contact information, or knowingly collect personal information from children through gameplay. It has no public profiles or chat. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact the developer so the message can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate. This policy does not impose an additional minimum age for offline play.

Security and policy changes

Please protect your device and take care when sharing screenshots or support attachments. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. If NORI’s data practices change, this page and its update date will be revised. Questions about this policy can be sent to Maksim Sokolov at msokolov2589@gmail.com.

Добровольные обращения в поддержку

Хранение и удаление

Локальные игровые данные сохраняются, чтобы питомец и прогресс оставались доступны при возвращении. В текущем приложении нет экранов сброса, экспорта или удаления аккаунта. Удаление приложения, в отличие от его сгрузки, обычно удаляет локальные данные; общий контейнер может сохраняться, пока им пользуется другое установленное приложение той же App Group. Отдельные копии могут оставаться в резервных копиях Apple по вашим настройкам. Разработчик не имеет доступа к вашему локальному сохранению и резервным копиям Apple, не может удалить их удалённо и не может восстановить удалённое сохранение со своего сервера. Переписка поддержки хранится столько, сколько необходимо для обработки обращения, необходимых записей или исполнения закона.
